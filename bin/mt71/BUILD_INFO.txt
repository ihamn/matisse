mt71 change (one, in slide.c):
  Stage-aware mt51 mid-burst abort.
  - R stage: abort on CapEff full (real_cred switched) or root_seen.
  - C stage: abort on euid==0 (cred switched) or root_seen; do NOT abort on
    CapEff full because C runs after R and CapEff is already full.
  Rationale: C previously fired all 6 shots without a landing signal, so any
  successful cred write could be clobbered by later shots (or simply not
  detected). This gives C the same early-stop protection R already had.
